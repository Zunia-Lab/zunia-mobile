Zunia is a self-custody wallet for the Cosmos ecosystem. Your recovery phrase is created on your phone, encrypted with your password, and never leaves the device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Hold and follow tokens on 220+ Cosmos networks, including Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Celestia, Akash and Safrochain.
• Send and receive with QR codes, an address book and payment links.
• Swap tokens through Osmosis routes. The route and every fee, including Zunia's 0.5% swap fee, are shown before you confirm.
• Move tokens between chains over IBC.
• Stake with validators, claim rewards and follow your positions.
• Vote on governance proposals.
• See your collectibles (NFTs) and a readable history of every transaction.

CONNECT TO APPS
• Open web apps in the built-in browser. They find Zunia the way they find a wallet extension, and every request appears as a native approval sheet.
• Connect a website on your computer by scanning its QR code. Both screens show the same six digits, so you know you are pairing with the right site. Messages travel end-to-end encrypted, and you approve each transaction on your phone.

BUILT FOR SAFETY
• Zunia asks for your password before every signature. Face ID or your fingerprint can unlock the wallet.
• Before you sign, Zunia decodes the transaction and shows what it does, on which network, and the fee.
• Alerts for incoming transfers and other wallet activity.
• No account, no tracking, no ads.
• Open source under the Apache 2.0 license.

Keep your recovery phrase offline. Anyone who has it controls your funds, and Zunia cannot recover it for you. Zunia never asks for it outside the wallet's own backup screens.
