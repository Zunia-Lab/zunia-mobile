Zunia is a self-custody wallet for the Cosmos ecosystem. Your keys are created or restored on this iPhone, sealed with your password and kept here. There is no Zunia account and no sign-up. Zunia never holds your funds and cannot recover a lost recovery phrase.

One wallet for Cosmos networks
• More than 220 Cosmos mainnet networks in the built-in catalog. Safrochain, Cosmos Hub and Osmosis are on at setup. Turn on the others you use, or add a network by hand.
• One portfolio for every network you turn on, with indicative prices in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY or CHF.
• Hide amounts with one tap.
• Several accounts, each with its own recovery phrase, all unlocked by one password.

Send across chains
• Send any token on the same chain, or to another chain over IBC.
• Before you sign, Zunia checks the route: the channel is open and both ends are active.
• Receive with a QR code, or put a token, an amount and a memo into a payment request.
• Keep the addresses you use often in an address book.

Swap
• Swap tokens through Osmosis, also when your tokens sit on another chain.
• Each swap made on the Swap screen carries a 0.5% Zunia fee, paid in the token you sell. The review shows it before you sign.
• If a swap that starts on another chain fails on Osmosis, the amount swapped comes back to you. The fee does not.

Stake and vote
• Stake with a validator, unstake, and claim your rewards.
• Read proposals and vote Yes, No, Veto or Abstain.
• Zunia takes no cut of staking rewards.

Use dApps
• Browse dApps inside Zunia. A site asks to connect for the networks it needs, and you approve each one.
• Pair with a website by scanning its QR code. Both screens show the same 6-digit code, and messages are end-to-end encrypted through Zunia's relay.
• Every request opens the same review: what it does, the fee, and warnings for lookalike sites, first-time recipients and messages Zunia cannot decode.

Keep it locked
• Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your password.
• Auto-lock after 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes.
• Turn on "Password on every signature" to confirm each transaction again.
• The recovery phrase is hidden while the screen is recorded or mirrored, and covered in the app switcher.
• A copied phrase is cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
• Your phrase stays in the Keychain on this device only. It is not copied to iCloud.

Stay informed
• Activity for your networks, with the details of each transaction.
• Notifications for tokens arriving, rewards to claim, unbonding and proposals. Zunia raises them itself while it is open and unlocked. No push server is involved.
• See and send your NFTs.

Fees
Zunia has no subscription, no premium tier and no token. Every transaction pays a network fee to the chain, shown before you sign. Sends, IBC transfers, staking and votes carry no Zunia fee. Only swaps made on Zunia's Swap screen carry the 0.5% Zunia fee.

One phrase, browser and phone
The same recovery phrase gives the same addresses in the Zunia browser extension. Each device keeps its own encrypted copy. Nothing syncs through a server.

Open source
The app's code is public at github.com/Zunia-Lab, under the Apache 2.0 license.

Keep your recovery phrase offline. Zunia will never ask for it, by email, in a chat or anywhere else.
